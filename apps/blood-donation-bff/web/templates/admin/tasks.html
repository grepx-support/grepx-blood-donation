How planning works

  1. 1Create a drive, and the 28-task checklist is added with due dates counted back from the drive date.
  2. 2Assign each task to anyone (admin, volunteer or donor), or leave it Unassigned so a volunteer can Pick up it.
  3. 3Move cards To do → In progress → Done. Stuck? Use Blocked and say why in the details.
  4. 4Owners get reminders (email & WhatsApp, important ones also by SMS) within 24 h of the due time until the task is done.
  5. 5Every change is kept in the task's history; after the drive, write the report & retro and turn its actions into tasks for next time.
